Good questions

Before you get started.

What are custom lapel pins?

Custom lapel pins are small wearable badges made to a particular design, such as a logo, emblem or illustration. They can identify a group, mark an achievement or become merchandise, and are commonly worn on jackets, uniforms and bags.

What is the difference between soft and hard enamel?

Soft enamel has recessed colour with raised metal outlines you can feel. Hard enamel is filled and polished to create a smoother surface, with colour and metal sitting level. The best option depends on your artwork and preferred texture.

How much do custom lapel pins cost?

Cost depends on quantity, size, production method, design complexity, metal finish, attachments and packaging. Tooling, artwork requirements and freight can also affect the total. Request an itemised quote for your design rather than relying on a generic unit price.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

The minimum needs to be confirmed for your design and production method. Include your approximate quantity in the enquiry so the available options can be checked.

How long does production and delivery take?

Timing depends on artwork approval, specifications, production capacity and delivery location. Share your required arrival date at the enquiry stage. Production and freight time must be confirmed before you commit to an order.

Can I use my own logo or artwork?

Yes, your own artwork can be the starting point for a custom pin. Use artwork you own or have permission to reproduce. Vector files are helpful; fine text, gradients and narrow details may need adjustment for the chosen process.

What if I only have an idea?

Describe the idea, who the pins are for and the look you want. A rough sketch or visual reference can help define the brief. Artwork support, fees and revision arrangements should be agreed with your quote.

What shapes, sizes, colours and metal finishes are available?

Pins can use standard or custom outlines, subject to manufacturing limits. Enamel suits separate areas of solid colour; printing can suit gradients and detailed images. Gold-tone, silver-tone, copper, black nickel and antique effects are options to discuss. Final availability and colour matching depend on the process.

Which attachments and packaging options are available?

Butterfly clutches and magnetic fittings are options to discuss, along with backing cards and presentation boxes. The best fitting depends on the pin size and how it will be worn. Confirm compatibility, availability and any additional cost in your quote.

Can pins be delivered throughout Australia?

Include your Australian postcode so delivery availability, freight cost and timing can be confirmed. Regional and remote destinations may require different delivery arrangements.
