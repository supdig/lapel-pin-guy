About The Lapel Pin Guy

An idea you can wear.

The Lapel Pin Guy is focused on helping customers explore custom pins for brands, communities and meaningful occasions.

A thoughtful starting point

The right details
make the difference.

A good pin starts with a clear purpose. It might bring a club together, mark a service milestone or turn an illustration into a collectible.

This website brings the main pin styles and design considerations into one place so you can prepare a useful enquiry. The specifications, availability and commercial terms for each project are confirmed through a quote.

Custom pins come first.

Explore enamel, die struck metal and printed approaches, with fittings and packaging considered as part of the brief. Related custom keyrings and badges can also be discussed as secondary enquiries, subject to availability.

For Australian projects.

Tell us your delivery location and required date so freight and timing can be checked. An Australian service focus does not mean products are manufactured in Australia. Ask for the manufacturing origin if it matters to your project.
