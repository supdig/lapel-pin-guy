Your enquiry essentials

A useful brief
starts here.

Share what you know. Mark uncertain details as flexible so they can be discussed.

  • Purpose, audience and any design references
  • Logo, sketch or description of your idea
  • Approximate quantity and preferred dimensions
  • Pin type, metal finish and colour references, if known
  • Fitting and packaging preferences
  • Required arrival date and delivery postcode
  • Budget guidance, if you have it
Before you approve

A few useful checks.

What should I check before artwork approval?

Check the spelling, dimensions, colours, metal finish, fitting and packaging against the written quote. A screen image cannot fully reproduce a physical metal or enamel finish.

What should the quote confirm?

The quantity, specifications, setup and artwork charges, freight, tax treatment, payment terms, approval process and estimated schedule should be clear before an order proceeds. Ask how changes, faults and delivery issues are handled.

What file should I prepare?

A vector file such as PDF, AI or EPS is a useful starting point for logos. A high-resolution image or sketch can help explain an idea. The required production files and any artwork fees need to be confirmed for your project.
